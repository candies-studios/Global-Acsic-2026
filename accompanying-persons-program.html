OVERVIEW

About the Event

The Global Symposium on Credit Guarantee & 38th ACSIC Conference 2026 will serve as a collaborative platform where global stakeholders come together to examine how credit guarantee mechanisms influence access to finance and long-term economic stability.

The symposium brings diverse perspectives—spanning policy, finance, and industry—to explore how evolving credit guarantee models support entrepreneurship, strengthen MSMEs, and respond to emerging economic challenges. Alongside knowledge exchange, the event is designed to foster meaningful connections among participants from different countries and sectors.
