Welcome Message

Message from the Chief Organizer

On behalf of CGTMSE, the host institution, I am pleased to welcome you to the Global Symposium on Credit Guarantee & 38th ACSIC Conference 2026, to be held on April 23-24 in Mumbai. We are honored to bring together global policymakers, financial institutions, practitioners, and thought leaders on a common platform to engage in meaningful dialogue on the evolving role of credit guarantee mechanisms.

In a rapidly changing financial landscape, credit guarantees remain a critical tool for enhancing access to finance, strengthening risk-sharing frameworks, and supporting enterprise-led growth. As innovation continues to reshape financial systems, this symposium provides an important opportunity to exchange global perspectives, share best practices, and explore forward-looking solutions.

I am confident that the collective expertise of our delegates will generate meaningful discussions and actionable insights. I also invite you to experience Mumbai's vibrant cultural, historical, and entrepreneurial character during your stay, and to forge meaningful connections with fellow participants and leaders in the global financial ecosystem.

I wish you a truly rewarding experience at the Global Symposium & 38th ACSIC Conference 2026, and look forward to your valued contributions towards advancing more inclusive and sustainable financial systems globally.

Shri Manish Sinha
Chief Executive Officer
Credit Guarantee Trustee Company (India) Limited (CGTMSE)

About the Symposium

The Global Symposium & 38th ACSIC Conference 2026 on Credit Guarantee will be held on April 23-24, 2026 at The Taj Mahal Palace, Mumbai.

This international conference emphasizes credit guarantees as essential mechanisms in modern finance, facilitating meaningful dialogue among policymakers, financial institutions, and practitioners on evolving credit guarantee approaches and global best practices.
