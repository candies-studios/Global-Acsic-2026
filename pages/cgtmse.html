CGTMSE

Credit Guarantee Fund Trust for Micro and Small Enterprises (India)

The Credit Guarantee Fund Trust for Micro and Small Enterprises (CGTMSE) is a national-level credit guarantee mechanism established to enhance access to formal finance for micro and small enterprises in India.

By providing guarantee cover on collateral-free loans, CGTMSE reduces the credit risk faced by lending institutions and encourages the flow of institutional credit to enterprises that may otherwise face financing constraints. The scheme supports entrepreneurship, strengthens financial inclusion, and contributes to employment generation by enabling enterprises to grow without the burden of asset-backed lending requirements.

CGTMSE represents a critical pillar of India’s credit support framework and plays a significant role in building a resilient and inclusive MSME ecosystem.
