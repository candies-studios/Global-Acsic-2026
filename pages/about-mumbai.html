About Mumbai

About Mumbai – Host City Guide

The City at a Glance

Mumbai is India’s financial capital and one of its most cosmopolitan cities. 
Set along the Arabian Sea, it combines historic architecture, global business districts, vibrant street life, and a thriving cultural scene.

Nearby Attractions

Located within close proximity to the event venue:

  • Gateway of India – Iconic waterfront landmark
  • Colaba Causeway – Shopping, cafés, and local markets
  • Marine Drive – Ocean-facing boulevard known as the “Queen’s Necklace”
  • Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Terminus – UNESCO World Heritage Site
  • Elephanta Caves – Ancient rock-cut caves (short ferry ride)

Local Cuisine & Dining Experiences

Mumbai offers one of the most diverse food cultures in India.

Popular Local Specialties:

Vada Pav
Pav Bhaji
Bhel Puri and Sev Puri
Masala Chai

From street food to fine dining, the city caters to all tastes, with international cuisines readily available near Colaba

Culture, Language & Etiquette

  • Mumbai is open, diverse, and welcoming
  • English is widely spoken, especially in professional and hospitality settings
  • Smart casual attire is appropriate; modest clothing is appreciated in traditional spaces

Safety & Practical Information

  • Mumbai is considered one of India’s safer metropolitan cities
  • South Mumbai has strong security presence
  • Emergency Number: 112
