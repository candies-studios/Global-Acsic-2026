Overview

About the Event

The Global Symposium on Credit Guarantee & 38th ACSIC Conference 2026 will serve as a collaborative platform where global stakeholders come together to examine how credit guarantee mechanisms influence access to finance and long-term economic stability.

The symposium brings diverse perspectives—spanning policy, finance, and industry—to explore how evolving credit guarantee models support entrepreneurship, strengthen MSMEs, and respond to emerging economic challenges. Alongside knowledge exchange, the event is designed to foster meaningful connections among participants from different countries and sectors.

What The Event Includes

Expert Address

Senior leaders and policymakers will offer strategic insights into how credit guarantee systems are evolving within global financial ecosystems.

Panel Conversations

Multi-sector panels will address themes of financial inclusion, economic resilience and access to credit through structured dialogue and expert perspectives.

Global Insights Session

A high-level session featuring international perspectives and cross-country experiences, fostering knowledge exchange on best practices, emerging trends and strategic innovations in credit guarantee frameworks.
